What is Founder's Plot?

Founder's Plot: Orchard Tycoon is a free orchard business game for iPhone and Android. You start with one apple tree and build a real farm business, in real dollars.

You grow apples, turn them into juice, jam, cider and more in your Processing Shed, and sell them in your own Orchard Store and at a weekend farmers market where shoppers come in waves, haggle over prices and send orders all week. Every price is in real dollars, so the choices feel like running a small business: what to grow, what to make, what to charge.

What's in the game

  • Grow. Plant Fuji trees, water and compost them, and harvest. Every harvest is graded A, B or C; better care means more Grade A apples, which are worth more. Weather and pests come and go, and seasons follow the real calendar.
  • Make. The Processing Shed turns apples into 8 products: Fresh-Pressed Juice, Applesauce, Apple Butter, Pie Filling, Sparkling Cider, Apple Cider Vinegar, Dried Apple Rings and Apple Jam, each with three levels of equipment. Players can discover their own house recipes, and Your Books shows what each product costs to make and what the business owns.
  • Sell. Your own Orchard Store sells to a limited number of shoppers each day while the player sets the prices and grows the building. Every Saturday and Sunday the farmers market opens: shoppers arrive in waves, buyers make offers to take or counter, free samples bring people in, and special market days give out ribbons. During the week, shoppers from the weekend send orders.
  • Grow the business. The Orchard Office signs standing weekly orders with restaurants, gift shops and wholesale buyers. Neighbors, daily tasks, 41 achievements and a weekly in-game newspaper about the player's own week round it out.
