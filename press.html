What is Founder's Plot?

Founder's Plot is a mobile orchard-economy game from Long Watch LLC. You start with one small plot of land and build it into a working business: growing and harvesting crops, processing them into higher-margin goods, and selling them into a live in-game market. The game is built around a full economic loop — grow, process, sell — rather than a single core action repeated on a timer.

The progression is grounded in real business mechanics: raw goods sell for far less than processed ones, equipment tiers gate what you can make, and expanding into new distribution channels is a deliberate, costed decision rather than an automatic unlock.

About the studio

Long Watch LLC is an independent game studio built around Founder's Plot as its first title. The game is designed, written, and developed by a small, tightly-scoped team based in Wichita, Kansas, with a focus on getting the core economic systems right before adding surface-level content.
