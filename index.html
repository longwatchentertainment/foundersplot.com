Real economics

Honest money.

Raw apples sell for $0.40 each. Process them into juice and earn $3.74 a bottle. The margin teaches you more than any tutorial ever could.

Processing

Higher margins, one step up.

Raw apples are just the start. Press them into juice, cook them into jam, and watch the same harvest sell for multiples more — if you've got the equipment tier to back it up.

Community

Demand-driven roadmap.

When enough players hit the same equipment limit, the community funds a new facility — and we build it. Your investment shapes what ships next.

A game that respects
how business actually works.

Most farming games give you an idle loop and call it a business. Founder's Plot gives you cash flow problems, reputation risk, and the specific satisfaction of watching a wholesale contract pay off after three weeks of careful planning.

You can go bankrupt. You can recover. The post-mortem tells you exactly where the money went and what to do differently. That's a feature, not a bug.

Built by a solo developer, on evenings, over two years. Designed to be the game that didn't exist yet.
