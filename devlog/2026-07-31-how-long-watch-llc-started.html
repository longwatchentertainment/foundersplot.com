Dev Log #1: How Long Watch LLC Actually Started

July 31, 2026

Hey, I'm Charles. Solo dev on Founder's Plot, a mobile business sim about starting with one backyard apple tree and building it into a real, vertically integrated food business. I wanted the first thing I ever post here to be honest about where the last couple months actually went, because it wasn't gameplay. It was paperwork.

Before I could write a line of game code that I felt good about shipping, I had to get the boring stuff right. A real company. A real bank account. A real developer account. Here's what that actually looked like.

Forming the LLC. Long Watch LLC is registered in Kansas, with a registered agent on file. Not glamorous, but it's the thing everything else hangs off of. No LLC means no EIN, no business bank account, and no way to sign developer agreements as a company instead of as a person.

EIN and banking. Once the LLC paperwork cleared, getting an EIN from the IRS was quick and free, about ten minutes. That let me open a Mercury business checking account under the LLC's name, which is where all the real Founder's Plot money (what little there is right now) actually lives.

The D-U-N-S number. This is one of those things nobody tells you about until you need it. A D-U-N-S number is basically an ID number for a business, and certain enterprise account setups require one. I applied for one, got it back in a few weeks, and it's still slowly propagating through some of the systems that check for it.

The Apple saga. This was the real time sink. My developer account started as an individual account, and converting it to a proper organization account under Long Watch LLC turned into a multi step migration. That included a domain ownership verification mismatch that needed its own support escalation to sort out. It got resolved, but it ate real calendar time I'd rather have spent building. If you're a solo dev reading this before you've hit this wall yourself, start the Apple org migration before you think you need it, not after.

Domains and the studio site. foundersplot.com and longwatchentertainment.com are both live now, registered through Cloudflare. Long Watch Entertainment has a small studio page up with the entity, formation, and location info, plus a link over to Founder's Plot. Founder's Plot has a placeholder landing page with a privacy policy and terms of service already posted, because I'd rather get that boring compliance stuff done early than scramble for it later.

Where things stand. Google Play Console and Apple Developer Program are both enrolled under the LLC now. The operating agreement is signed. The GitHub org is up. None of this is exciting to look at, but it's the foundation the actual game gets built on top of. And building on a shaky foundation is exactly the kind of shortcut this game's own bankruptcy mechanic punishes, so I figured I probably shouldn't take it in real life either.

Next time: what the game actually looks like right now, and the first real mechanic I got working, a backyard apple tree that grows, gets thirsty, gets sick, and needs you to actually take care of it.

Charles
