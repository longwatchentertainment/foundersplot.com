Dev Log #16: The Farmers Market Gets Busy

November 13, 2026

Sign in with Apple and Google is live. Last week I told you version 1.1.0 was waiting on review. It went live on Google Play on November 7 and on the App Store on November 10. As of today 1.1.0 is the oldest version that can play, so if your game says "Update required", grab the update from your store and you will be right back on your farm.

The bigger news: the new Farmers Market is on for everyone, starting today. I spent this week turning the market from one button into something you actually run. Here is what you will find.

  • Market events. Every market day now has an event that every player shares, and the first ones show up this weekend, November 14 and 15. Some change which products sell best. Some come with a goal for the day that wins a ribbon and 25 coins.
  • Shoppers come through the day. Instead of one Collect button, shoppers now arrive in five waves from morning to evening. Tap Serve Shoppers when they show up. Anyone you miss waits until midnight.
  • You set your prices. Each product can go from 70% to 140% of the suggested price. Price high and fewer people buy. Price low and more people stop by.
  • Shoppers who haggle. Bargain hunters, restaurant owners, gift shoppers and others may ask for a bulk deal. You can accept, counter or decline. Push too hard and they may walk away.
  • Free samples. Put out 1 to 5 samples of a product from your stock and it draws more interest for the rest of the day. The sample card now also shows about what each sample costs you to make.
  • Orders between markets. During the week, shoppers from your booth may send you an order by letter, phone or note. If one runs out of time, you can get 24 more hours once by watching an ad or spending 10 coins.
  • Booth decorations. Balloons, bunting, pumpkins and more, with painted art. The name banner unlocks when branding does, after 20 batches, so it can carry your business name.

A few optional extras cost one ad or some coins, like calling back a shopper who walked away. None of them sell you a win.

A cleaner Market screen. Prices and stock now live in one window, the deal shoppers are grouped together, and your booth opens in its own window. There is also a new "How the market works" guide. One player told me owning tables versus renting space was confusing, so the guide and the screen now spell it out, and you can rent space for another table in the middle of a weekend.

An iPhone fix. On my own iPhone, the coin buttons inside the deal window did nothing, and then other popups stopped showing until a restart. That is fixed.

For new players. One player opened the game six times and never found the Harvest button, because it sat below the first screen. The Getting Started card now has its own Harvest button, and the Processing Shed opens after your fifth harvest.

Coming soon: version 1.1.1. It is in review at both stores now. The game's pictures shrink from about 84 MB to about 17 MB, so the download is much smaller. A brand new install will also wait a few seconds on first launch to pick up the latest update, instead of starting on an older copy. I will tell you here when it is out.

Next week I am mostly watching. This is the first weekend of the new market, so I want to see how it plays for you. If something feels off, tell me.

Charles
