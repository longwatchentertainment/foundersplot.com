Dev Log #6: The Debug Screen Finally Let Go

September 4, 2026

Every dev log so far has come with the same asterisk: it works, but you'd never see it, because the whole game has been running through one giant debug menu since the first playable build. That changed this week. There's a real bottom tab bar now, and the Orchard is the first piece to move out of the debug screen into an actual player-facing tab, plots and watering and harvesting and weather and all. Processing and the Market are next, same debug-screen-to-real-tab move, just not done yet. It's not a flashy change to describe, but it's the one that makes everything else this year has built start to feel like an actual app instead of a spreadsheet with buttons.

With that plumbing in place I went back and filled in some real gaps. The orchard now has a second, third, and fourth plot to buy, so you're not stuck babying two trees forever, and there are two permanent upgrades, drip irrigation and a compost system, that slow down watering chores and passively heal your soil over time. Processing got a second queue slot so you can run two batches at once instead of babysitting one at a time, and every equipment upgrade past your starting gear now takes real installation time instead of being instant, matching how the weather-mitigation buildings already worked. I also went through the story beats that fire as you hit harvest milestones and found five of them still showing a "not built yet" placeholder for mechanics that had actually shipped weeks ago. Fixed the flags, and gave harvest 8 a real coin reward instead of just a pat on the back.

The bigger addition is the start of the referral system. There's a shareable link now, and if you invite someone who actually starts playing and reaches Tier 2, you both get a coin bonus once you claim it. I also built the other half of that GDD feature, a "Founding Farmer" badge for the first 500 players, so if you're reading this and playing the app early, that's for you. Password reset also finally works the right way, through a real link on foundersplot.com instead of a broken email pointing nowhere, and there's a native rating prompt now that shows up at reasonable moments, like your second achievement, instead of nagging you on day one.

I'm being upfront that none of this has touched a real phone yet. Last week's lesson was that the gap between "typechecks clean" and "feels good in your hand" is bigger than it looks, and this week has more surface area than any single week so far: new tabs, new purchases, new timers, a whole referral loop with two devices talking to a server. So next week is testing week, working through all of it on-device before I let myself add anything new. If something here ends up shaky when I actually play it, that's expected, and I'd rather tell you that now than pretend everything above is bulletproof.

Charles
