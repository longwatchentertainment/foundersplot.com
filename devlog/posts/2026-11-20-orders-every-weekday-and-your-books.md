# Dev Log #17: Orders Every Weekday, and Your Books

**Orders now come every weekday.** A player told me their orders stopped coming on Tuesday. Before, a good weekend brought a handful of orders for the whole week, and once they arrived the card went quiet with no explanation. That is no fun, so I changed it:

- **1 to 3 orders every weekday.** How many depends on how your last market weekend went: a quiet weekend brings 1 a day, an average one 2 a day and a great one 3 a day. They arrive between 8 am and 6 pm Central. If you skip the market or sell nothing, there are no orders that week.
- **Sized to your booth.** Each order asks for anywhere from 1 up to half of what your booth can sell in a day, so a bigger booth gets bigger orders. The old weekly limit is gone.
- **2 extra orders a day.** Asking your shoppers for another order, with an ad or coins, now works twice a day instead of twice a week.
- **The card tells you what's coming.** When nothing is open it says another order is on the way, and when the week's orders are done it says so.

The extra orders and the end of the weekly limit work now. The new daily orders start with the week after this weekend's market, November 21 and 22.

**Your Books.** There is a new page that shows what your business really earns. Find it under your apples in the Orchard, in the Processing Shed and on market days. It shows your sales, what the things you sold cost you to make, your profit, free samples as a marketing cost, what your stock is worth, how your last harvest and batches were costed, and how much wear each piece of equipment has left. Costs show in brackets, the way an accountant writes them. Every grade of apple costs the same to grow, since raw apples sell for the same price whatever their grade. Grade only matters once apples are made into something. Free Samples and Prices and Stock now use these real costs too.

**Smaller fixes at the market:**

- A shopper you paid to find, call back or ask for won't walk away on your next counter. They come up part of the way instead.
- Free Samples no longer lets you pick more than one grade holds, so the line always matches what you give away.
- If you've never set your own prices, a one-time tip after a sales summary (from your second market weekend) points you to Prices and Stock.

**A fix for iPhone.** On iOS 26, the back button on the second guide page you opened (How to Play or How the Market Works) could stop working. Both guides now use the game's own back button.

**One quick question.** The first time you open the game on a day after your first, it asks once how you found Founder's Plot. You can skip it, but an answer helps me know where to share the game next.

**What's next.** This weekend's market is the first that leads into daily orders. I'll look back at how the week went and tune anything that needs it. If orders feel too big, too small or too many, let me know. Your feedback helps more than anything.
