# Dev Log #7: Every Screen Got a Real Building

This week didn't add a single new mechanic. No new recipe, no new upgrade, no new economy lever. Instead I spent the whole week making the game look like the game I actually designed instead of the placeholder shapes and debug menus it's been running on underneath, and I think it was worth it.

The biggest visible change is that the Processing Shed now has a front door. Instead of opening the Processing tab straight into a wall of machines and recipes, you land on a Facilities screen first, a little illustrated shed that shows its door open when something's cooking inside and closed when it's empty. Tap it and you walk into the shed itself. It sounds small, but it's the difference between "here's a dashboard of processing equipment" and "here's your farm, and this is one of the buildings on it." Orchard, Market, and Account all got the same treatment over the past couple weeks, real illustrated hero art, real product and equipment images instead of icons, a shared button and card style so every screen finally feels like it belongs to the same game. This week Processing was the last domain to catch up.

The other thing you'll notice, if you've played much, is that popups look different now. Every "are you sure you want to sell these apples" or "batch complete, collect your jam" message used to be a plain gray system dialog, the kind any app on your phone throws up. Those are gone. Confirmations and choices now show up on an illustrated parchment panel with a wood-and-gold trim, centered on screen instead of sliding up from the bottom, because that read more like an actual message from the farm than a phone alert. And for the quick stuff, batch started, apples sold, that doesn't need you to tap anything, there's now a small illustrated banner that fades in at the top and disappears on its own instead of stopping you cold with a dialog box.

I'll be honest that this last part had a rough patch. The first time I actually saw the new toast banner on my phone instead of just in the code, it was rendering way too big, bleeding off both edges of the screen with text riding up over the status bar. Nobody had actually looked at it on a real device before that. Fixed it, but it's a good reminder that "compiles and looks right in my head" and "looks right in your hand" are still two different things, even for something this small. I'm also chasing a rare black-screen report tied to one of these new popups that I haven't fully nailed down yet, so if anything ever briefly flashes black when you dismiss a confirmation, that's a known issue I'm still tracking, not something you did wrong.

Nothing new to build with this week, but the whole game reads a lot more like a finished product now than it did seven days ago. Next up is finishing the polish pass and getting back to on-device testing before any new features go in.

Charles
