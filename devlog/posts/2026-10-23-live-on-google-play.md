# Dev Log #13: Founder's Plot Is Live On Google Play

The big news this week is simple. Founder's Plot went live on Google Play on October 20. I submitted it, Google approved it in about a day, and it rolled out to 177 countries. The first two real players showed up that same day, which means the game now has players that are not me, my test accounts, or an app reviewer. Two people is a tiny number, and I am happy about every one of them.

The iPhone version is a different story for now. I submitted it to Apple on October 18, and it is waiting for review. I set it to release manually, so when Apple approves it I choose the moment. I will tell you here the day it happens.

**Why I did not wait longer.** I had planned to submit around the second week of November. Then the testing passes kept coming back clean, so I moved it up. The new player path now clears all eight Getting Started steps in about 11 harvests, and nothing from the last round was still open. I would rather ship a smaller, honest first version and keep fixing it than keep polishing alone.

**Two bugs I am glad I caught before launch.**

The first one could have been ugly. If you signed in to an account on a phone that still had another account's save sitting on it, the game could upload that old save to your account. That is how coins could get cloned from one account to another. Now the game never uploads another account's save when you sign in. Your account is yours, and what is on the server is what counts.

The second one was about free test purchases. On test builds, buying a coin pack costs nothing. I did not want a tester's free purchase turning into real coins, so now only accounts on a short approved list get coins from those test purchases. Everyone else's test purchase is recorded and grants nothing. Real purchases are not affected.

**Plainer words, again.** Sign in, sign up, and reset link errors now say what went wrong in normal language. If a coin pack purchase is cancelled, unavailable, or fails, you get a different message for each one, because those are three very different situations. The Market lines about reputation and loyalty read more simply, and long button labels shrink to fit instead of getting cut off halfway through a word.

**A way to make sure everyone is current.** I added a switch that can ask players to update the app. I hope I never need it, but if I ever ship a version with a serious problem, I can ask people to update instead of letting them keep playing on a broken build.

**Where I could use your help.** You can find the game by searching for Founder's Plot: Orchard Tycoon on Google Play. A plain search for the name mostly shows a movie from 2016, so I am working on the website and the search listings too. If you play it, tell me what confused you. The first ten minutes matter most to me, and I can only see them clearly through your eyes.

Next up is waiting on Apple, watching how the first real players move through the early game, and fixing whatever they find.

Charles
