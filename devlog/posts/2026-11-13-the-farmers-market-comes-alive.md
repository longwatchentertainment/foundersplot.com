# Dev Log #16: The Farmers Market Comes Alive

**The new Farmers Market is on for everyone, starting this weekend.** A player told me last Saturday they expected the market to be more interactive. A market day used to be one tap of "Collect Today's Sales". I spent this week rebuilding it, and it went live for every player today.

Here is what you will find on the Market tab:

- **Shoppers arrive in waves.** Five times a day (8 am, 11 am, 2 pm, 5 pm and 8 pm Central) a new group shows up, and "Serve Shoppers" sells to everyone who is waiting. Shoppers who arrive while you are away wait until midnight.
- **You set your own prices.** "Prices and Stock" shows a suggested price for each product and a slider to go higher or lower. Above the suggestion fewer shoppers buy, below it more do. Prices go back to the suggestion each morning.
- **Haggling.** A wave can bring up to two shoppers, like a restaurant owner or a bargain hunter, who want a bulk deal on one product. The deal window shows their offer next to your own price and what the Processing Shed would pay. Accept, counter or say no, but push too hard and they may walk away.
- **Free samples.** Hand out up to five samples of a product from your own stock. That product gets more interest for the rest of the day and a few more shoppers stop by. Each sample card now shows about what it costs you to make, as well as what it would have sold for.
- **A shared event every market day.** Everyone gets the same event, like Pie Contest or Chef's Day, posted on the Tuesday before. Some have a goal for the day that wins a ribbon and 25 coins. The first events run this weekend, November 14 and 15.
- **Orders between markets.** In the week after a market, shoppers from your booth send orders by letter, phone message or note, priced from what sold at your tables. A better weekend brings more of them. Some are a simple yes or no, some want to haggle.
- **Booth decorations.** Balloons, bunting, pumpkins and more, bought with coins, for looks only. The Name Banner unlocks with branding, after 20 batches.

A few optional extras cost one ad or some coins, like calling back a shopper who walked away or giving an order more time. None of them buys a win.

**The Market tab is easier to read.** On my own iPhone the list of grades at the booth took far too much scrolling, so it now lives inside "Prices and Stock", where you tap a grade to keep it at home. The deal window used to be six sentences of numbers. It is now a small price table with one line telling you how the offer compares. I also fixed a bug where some coin buttons did nothing on iPhone.

**Getting Started ends with the Processing Shed.** The Shed now opens at the end of Getting Started, after your 5th harvest, with free Harvest Skips to cover the waits along the way.

**Version 1.1.1 is live on Google Play and in review on the App Store.** Sign in with Apple and Google (1.1.0) is now out on both stores. 1.1.1 shrinks the game's pictures from about 84 MB to about 17 MB. A brand new install now waits a few seconds on first launch so it starts on the latest update. You need 1.1.0 or newer to play now, so if the game asks you to update, that is why.

**What's next.** This is the first weekend with the whole new market running, and I can't wait to see how it plays. Next week I'll look back at how the weekend went and tune anything that needs it. If a deal felt unfair or something was confusing, let me know. Your feedback helps more than anything.

Charles
