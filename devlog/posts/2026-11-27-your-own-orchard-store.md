# Dev Log #18: Your Own Orchard Store

This was a big week for the game. Here is what changed.

**The Orchard Store.** The Sell buttons are gone. No real business can sell an endless pile of apples the moment they come off the tree, so now you sell from your own store. The Processing tab is now called Facilities, and the Orchard Store is its first building, open from your first minute. Stock the shelves and shoppers come by through the day to buy at the everyday price. Grade B and C apples lose less of their price here than at the market. You can grow the store (floor space, a better sign, longer hours, more parking, a tour bus) and decorate it with coins. Nothing in your stock was lost, and the market and orders work just as before.

**The Orchard Office.** Once you've earned $2,500 and made 3 different products, the Orchard Office opens on the Facilities tab. Restaurants, gift shops and the market's wholesale buyer who keep buying from you can offer a standing account: a set number of one product every week for 4 weeks, a little under the everyday price, however many shoppers come. Miss a week and it pays nothing; miss two and the account closes.

**Neighbors.** You now have up to 5 neighbors, other real players. Once a day you can visit each one and leave a note from a short list, and you both earn a coin. You can also lend a hand and water their thirstiest tree. If you'd rather keep your orchard to yourself, there's a switch on the Account tab.

**House recipes.** In the Processing Shed, the Recipe Book lets you add Cinnamon, Honey, Caramel, Ginger or Vanilla to a batch. Find a pairing that works and you unlock your own house version of that product, named after your orchard. It's made from Grade A apples and sells for 25% more. Your usual recipe stays too.

**A reason to drop by every day.** Today's tasks gives you three small goals a day, and finishing all three on days in a row grows your bonus. Today's tasks and Neighbors fold up when you tap their headers. Every Monday at noon Central, the Orchard Valley Gazette prints a short paper about your own week.

**Orders.** Orders are now priced at the normal market price plus the stars and regulars you've earned, sized to what you actually sold last weekend, with no more than 2 open for one product at a time. These apply to the orders that follow this weekend's market. Finished orders now fold under one line, so the list stays short.

**Smaller things:**

- You can name your orchard once Getting Started is done, or after your first market weekend.
- Start Over on the Account tab gives you a fresh orchard and keeps your coins.
- Your Books now has a Balance Sheet and a Cash Flow page.
- A player asked for every care button back: the Account tab has a "Show every tree button" switch.
- A player asked how to see their ribbons: tapping "Your prizes" lists every one.
- For a while, ads on iPhone weren't opening. That's fixed.
- Lots of new achievements.

**The County Fair is coming.** Entries for the Winter Preserves Fair open December 19, and the fair weekend is January 9 and 10. Ribbons pay cash prizes, and Best in Show pays the most.

**What's next.** Version 1.1.2 is built and heading to both stores for review. It brings a proper iPad layout and a little vibration for harvests, sales and rewards. I'll look back at how the store's first week went and tune anything that needs it. If it feels too slow or too quick, tell me. Your feedback helps more than anything.

Charles
