Dev Log #14: Founder's Plot Is Now Live On Both App Stores

October 30, 2026

Founder's Plot is now live in both app stores. Last week I told you I was waiting on Apple. That wait ended on October 28, so the game is now on the App Store for iPhone as well as on Google Play for Android. Thank you to everyone who has played so far, and if you were waiting for the iPhone version, it is ready for you.

This week was mostly about the early game. I watched how the first real players move through it and spent the week sanding off the spots where people could get confused or accidentally do something they did not mean to.

Sorting apples by grade is easier to see. The Processing Shed now shows how many apples you have of each grade right on the grade buttons, so you can see at a glance whether you have enough A grade apples before you start a batch. The recipe picker does the same thing. It shows how many of each product you have in stock, split by grade. Before this, you had to remember what you had, or go look. The grade line also always lists the grades you keep in A, B, C order now, which sounds tiny but made the screen much calmer to read.

Plain words for the second batch slot. It used to be called a "2nd Queue Slot" with a description that sounded like a spreadsheet. It is now the 2nd Batch Slot, and it says what it does: run two batches at the same time. If your slots are full, the message tells you that in normal language.

The game asks before it spends. Every purchase now has a confirmation step, including the buy and upgrade buttons in Product Inventory. I would rather add one extra tap than have anyone lose cash or coins to a slip of the thumb.

Getting Started points the way. The button you should tap next now pulses, so the early steps feel less like a maze. The Skip button that lets you watch an ad to skip the harvest wait glows while the timer runs, so it is easy to spot. The Margin card, which explains profit, now only shows for a player who has never sold anything. If you already know what a margin is, it stays out of your way. The "please rate the game" request also waits until you have unlocked the Processing Shed and played for two days. I did not want to ask for a review from someone still figuring out what a tree is.

Sign in with Apple and Google is coming. Several of you asked for this, and it was a good ask. I built it this week, but it is not released yet. When it ships, you will be able to continue with Apple on iPhone or Google on Android instead of making a password. On iPhone both options will show up together, because Apple requires that. If you ever delete your account, the game will also cut the link to your Apple sign in. It needs a new store build, so I will tell you here when it is out.

Better password messages. If your reset link has expired, the game now tells you what to do next. Passwords that are too short, or that have shown up in known data leaks, get a friendly explanation. The Set New Password screen now looks like the rest of Founder's Plot instead of a stray page.

Two new farmer portraits. One in a straw hat and red plaid, and one in a rhinestone cowboy hat and overalls. More faces on the farm.

I am also working on a build that supports iPad. It is not ready yet, but a bigger screen for the orchard is on the way.

Next week I want to keep watching the first minutes of play and fix whatever trips people up. If something confused you, tell me. That is the most useful thing anyone can do for this game right now.

Charles
